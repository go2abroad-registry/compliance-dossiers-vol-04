Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible only when the virtual office provider offers a physical reception desk, dedicated postal address, acceptance of legal service of process, telephone answering with local zone code, and maintenance of records at that address. A Quebec limited partnership must have a declared establishment address in Quebec under the Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises. A bare mail-forwarding service without physical staff and service-of-process acceptance is not acceptable. For UHT oat milk plant operations, a virtual office cannot substitute for the manufacturing facility address, which must be registered separately as an actual place of business.

Competent corporate registry

Governing statute

Civil Code of Québec (CQLR c CCQ-1991), arts. 2236–2250 (société en commandite / limited partnership); Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises (CQLR c P-44.1), ss. 1–31 and regulations; where the general partner is a body corporate, Quebec Business Corporations Act (CQLR c S-31.1).

Formation / constitutional filings

Déclaration d'immatriculation (Declaration of Registration) under the Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises, supplemented by the Déclaration d'établissement (Declaration regarding the place of business).

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Quebec limited partnership; capital may be fixed in cash, equipment or industrial assets (UHT line). CCQ arts. 2245 and 2249 prohibit limited partners from withdrawing contributions where the withdrawal would reduce the partnership's assets below the capital figure declared in the Declaration of Registration or render the partnership insolvent. No escrow account or bank block is required by statute.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the municipal fire prevention service (per the Quebec Fire Safety Code, c. B-1.1, r. 1) covering UHT tanks, the aseptic packaging hall, and ammonia/glycol cold rooms; structural load inspection certificate for the silo area and packaging mezzanine issued by a licensed engineer (Engineers Act, c. I-9) and filed with the municipal building department; occupational workplace clearance certificate from CNESST for the implementation of the prevention program, machine-guarding of the UHT homogenizers and CIP systems, lockout/tagout, and ergonomic load-station requirements.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Quebec limited partnership; capital may be fixed in cash, equipment or industrial assets (UHT line). CCQ arts. 2245 and 2249 prohibit limited partners from withdrawing contributions where the withdrawal would reduce the partnership's assets below the capital figure declared in the Declaration of Registration or render the partnership insolvent. No escrow account or bank block is required by statute.
