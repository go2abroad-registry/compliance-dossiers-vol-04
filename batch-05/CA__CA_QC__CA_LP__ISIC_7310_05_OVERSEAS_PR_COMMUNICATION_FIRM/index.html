Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ) – Registre des entreprises du Québec

Governing statute

Civil Code of Québec, articles 2236-2266 (limited partnership); Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises, CQLR c. P-44.1, sections 1-40 (registration and public disclosure)

Formation / constitutional filings

Declaration of Registration (Déclaration d'immatriculation) under the Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None – Quebec law imposes no minimum paid-up capital or escrow account requirement for limited partnerships; capital contributions are freely fixed in the partnership agreement and may be made in cash, kind, or industry, with the LP operable immediately upon registration.

Funding & unlock path: Certificate of occupancy issued by the competent Quebec municipality based on compliance with the Quebec Building Code (chapter I, Building) and the Safety Code (chapter I, Safety, r. 2.1), covering means of egress, fire detection and alarm systems, emergency lighting, and barrier-free access; annual fire-safety inspection by the local fire safety service; CNESST establishment registration and employer obligations under the Occupational Health and Safety Act (CQLR c. S-2.1) and the Act respecting industrial accidents and occupational diseases (CQLR c. A-3.001), including first-aid kits, worker health and safety training, and a written prevention program where applicable.

Paid-in capital rule: None – Quebec law imposes no minimum paid-up capital or escrow account requirement for limited partnerships; capital contributions are freely fixed in the partnership agreement and may be made in cash, kind, or industry, with the LP operable immediately upon registration.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, that the General Partner of [●] Limited Partnership (the "Firm") hereby authorizes the formation of the Firm as a limited partnership under the Civil Code of Québec and the filing of its Declaration of Registration with the Registraire des entreprises du Québec; FURTHER RESOLVED, that the capital contributions of the limited partners, in the amounts set forth in the Limited Partnership Agreement, are hereby accepted and subscribed; FURTHER RESOLVED, that the registered office of the Firm is established at [●], Québec, which address is hereby adopted as the Firm's head office and registered seat; FURTHER RESOLVED, that [●] is appointed as manager and duly authorized mandatory of the Firm for the conduct of its international media relations and crisis public relations business.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable – no industrial license is mandated for a public relations agency (NAICS 541820) under Quebec law; the relevant administrative bodies are the municipality for occupancy/zoning permits and the Régie du bâtiment du Québec for building code compliance.

Category: Not applicable – the only operational clearances are the municipal certificate of occupancy and, where an exterior sign is installed, a sign installation permit issued by the local borough or municipality.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Agence du revenu du Canada — federal GST/HST and income tax administration via Business Registration Online (BRO) and My Business Account portals; Revenu Québec administers provincial income tax, the Québec Sales Tax (QST), and GST/HST collection for Québec through the clicSÉCUR Affaires portal; entity-level registration is filed with the Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ), which issues the Québec Enterprise Number (NEQ).

E-government filing portal

Non-resident limited partners: (1) register the extra-provincial limited partnership (société en commandite) with the REQ to obtain the NEQ; (2) obtain the 9-digit CRA Business Number (BN) through BRO or Form RC1, filed by the general partner; (3) open the GST/HST account (RTxxxxxxx) with CRA; (4) register for QST (RQ account) with Revenu Québec; (5) file annual T5013 Partnership Information Return and Québec RL-15 slips; non-resident partners additionally require a Canadian tax number and may file under ITA section 216.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Non-resident limited partners: (1) register the extra-provincial limited partnership (société en commandite) with the REQ to obtain the NEQ; (2) obtain the 9-digit CRA Business Number (BN) through BRO or Form RC1, filed by the general partner; (3) open the GST/HST account (RTxxxxxxx) with CRA; (4) register for QST (RQ account) with Revenu Québec; (5) file annual T5013 Partnership Information Return and Québec RL-15 slips; non-resident partners additionally require a Canadian tax number and may file under ITA section 216.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Mandatory fiscal transparency under ITA s. 96; no check-the-box corporate election is available for a limited partnership under the Income Tax Act; available elections include: (i) non-resident partner election under ITA s. 216(1) to file net Canadian-source income returns; (ii) partnership fiscal period election under ITA s. 249.1; (iii) property rollover election under ITA s. 97(2) where partners transfer capital property into the LP; (iv) GST/HST joint election under ETA s. 156 for tax-exempt supplies between registrants; and (v) QST equivalent elections administered by Revenu Québec.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14.975%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 worldwide taxable supplies (excluding zero-rated exports) in the current calendar quarter and the preceding four consecutive quarters before GST/HST registration becomes mandatory (small supplier threshold); QST registration threshold is aligned with the GST/HST threshold for suppliers carrying on business in Québec.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

PR retainer, media relations, crisis communications, and associated advisory services supplied to a non-resident person are zero-rated under ETA Schedule VI Part V s. 7 (and QST equivalents), provided the service is not rendered to an individual physically present in Canada, does not relate to real property or tangible personal property situated in Canada at the time of supply, and is not for the benefit of a Canadian-resident person; advertising services directed at the Canadian market remain taxable even when supplied to a non-resident; zero-rating also applies to exported physical PR collateral under Schedule VI Part I; no statutory FX repatriation deadline exists under Canadian GST/QST law — the 180-day documentary benchmark is applied by banks and CRA/Revenu Québec audit practice for export evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (B13A) where physical goods accompany campaign deliverables
  • Signed client service agreement evidencing non-resident status and place of supply
  • Commercial invoice with explicit 'zero-rated export' notation and foreign client address
  • Bill of lading, air waybill, or courier receipt for physical media collateral
  • Inward remittance certificate, SWIFT MT103, or bank advice evidencing foreign-currency receipt
  • GST/HST registration status certificate of the foreign client, if any
  • Internal work papers demonstrating services were performed outside Canada and no benefit accrued to a Canadian-resident person

No continuous transaction control (CTC) or real-time fiscal clearance/digital stamping regime; Canada uses periodic self-assessment returns. Mandatory electronic filing of GST/HST and QST returns through Revenu Québec's GST/HST Internet File Transfer service and clicSÉCUR Affaires; registrants must retain all e-invoices and source documents in machine-readable format for six years; QR-code-billed simplified invoices are permitted for amounts under CAD 150; Québec additionally mandates electronic invoicing for provincial public-sector suppliers and enhanced audit-file readability standards.

CRA and Revenu Québec may reassess zero-rating claims within four years (indefinitely for misrepresentation), clawing back denied zero-rated status, ITCs, and QST input refunds with prescribed-rate interest compounded daily; gross negligence penalty under ETA s. 280(1) is the greater of CAD 250 and 25% of the net tax understated; false input tax credit/rebate claims attract the ETA s. 280(2) penalty of 25%; fraudulent export schemes are subject to criminal prosecution under ETA s. 327 and Criminal Code s. 380 with fines up to CAD 100,000 and imprisonment up to five years on indictment; directors, general partners, and knowingly participating limited partners may face joint and several liability for QST and GST arrears.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts; no exchange-control approval is required. Main constraints for a Quebec PR-firm LP are the Part XIII withholding at 25% (treaty-reduced) on guaranteed partner fees and certain non-resident remittances, and the Canadian income allocation of non-resident partners under section 96 of the Income Tax Act, which requires T5013 partnership returns. Profit remittance for a US/Europe-focused PR operation is otherwise unblocked.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canada imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export or service receipts; foreign-currency retainer proceeds may be retained indefinitely. Repatriation or conversion is subject only to FINTRAC EFT reporting at CAD 10,000 or more per incoming wire; no conversion ratio or statutory timeline applies.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada); FINTRAC as the AML transactional surveillance authority; Canada operates a free-float currency with no statutory FX licensing body under the Currency Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Constrained Medium. Wise, Revolut, Airwallex and Payoneer are legally usable as ancillary disbursement rails for sub-CAD 25,000 monthly retainer flows only if registered with FINTRAC as money-services businesses in Canada. They cannot act as the principal clearing bank for the LP because they are not Schedule I banks, do not clear through Payments Canada, and cannot hold a capital escrow designation. The treasury core must remain with a Quebec-licensed domestic bank; EMI balances should be swept monthly to the operating account.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Layered limited-partnership structure with opaque capital stack

Root cause: Layered limited-partnership structure with opaque capital stack

Defense evidence: Complete LP agreement under the Civil Code of Quebec (société en commandite), REQ registration certificate, full UBO schedule for all general and limited partners, organisational chart confirming the LP is not a conduit, and a legal opinion where a non-resident corporate partner exceeds 25% of capital.
Cross-border retainer wires above CAD 10,000 without economic substrate

Root cause: Cross-border retainer wires above CAD 10,000 without economic substrate

Defense evidence: Signed retainer agreements, media-plan purchase orders, invoice-to-settlement mapping, FINTRAC EFT reporting log, and bank reference letters from the foreign paying bank; all wires must carry a capital-contribution or services purpose code.
Negative news screening of PR clients or ultimate beneficiaries

Root cause: Negative news screening of PR clients or ultimate beneficiaries

Defense evidence: Date-stamped negative-media report generated within 30 days before onboarding, enhanced due-diligence file with authoritative references, senior-manager sign-off memorandum, and semi-annual PEP re-screen where any client falls into the PEP class.
Non-resident capital contribution without evidentiary trail

Root cause: Non-resident capital contribution without evidentiary trail

Defense evidence: Signed source-of-wealth certificate from each contributing partner, 12-month bank statements of the contributor, audited or GAAP-abridged financial statements of the LP, contribution promissory note, and a Part XIII tax-indemnity memorandum covering non-resident remittances.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103 from the foreign paying bank to a Quebec Schedule I bank (e.g., National Bank of Canada, Institution 006) through a correspondent such as BNY or JPM, with the Remittance Information narrative carrying the code CTRAT or EQUI and the text Contribution to partnership capital - no consideration. Domestic classification under the Canadian Banking Association purpose-code set should be CF. The benefit account is the LP operational or capital escrow sub-account; the receiving branch is pre-notified at T-1 for same-day contributions above CAD 1,000,000 via Payments Canada high-value clearing (LVTS/Lynx). Verification requires the signed capital-contribution resolution, 12-month contributor bank statements, and FINTRAC EFT records for every wire at CAD 10,000 or more.SWIFT MT103 remains the default for cross-border PR retainer and vendor settlements; the US-corridor uses ACH (SEC Code CTX) for low-value and Fedwire for high-value payments, while intra-Canada high-value uses Payments Canada LVTS/Lynx and low-value instantaneous transfers use the Real-Time Rail. A commercial letter of credit (MT700) is permissible for overseas media inventory purchases against a performance certificate rather than a transport document, but invoice-based disbursement through the treasury system is preferred for PR retainers.
