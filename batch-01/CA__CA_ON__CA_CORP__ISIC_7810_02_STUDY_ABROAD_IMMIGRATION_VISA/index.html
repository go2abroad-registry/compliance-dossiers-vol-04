E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election exists for Canadian business corporations; a corporation is automatically taxed at the entity level under Part I of the Income Tax Act. The available elections are computational rather than classificatory, including ITA section 85 rollovers and, for non-resident corporations, section 216 rental income treatment. A Canadian resident corporation cannot elect to be treated as a partnership or disregarded entity. Hybrid mismatch and FAPI anti-deferral rules apply automatically and cannot be elected around; there is no statutory hybrid mismatch election comparable to the U.S. check-the-box regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 in cumulative taxable supplies over the preceding four calendar quarters, being the small-supplier threshold; zero-rated export supplies count toward this threshold, and voluntary GST/HST registration is permitted.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Excise Tax Act, Schedule VI, a supply of services made to a non-resident person is zero-rated where the service is not in respect of Canadian real property, tangible personal property located in Canada, or a service rendered to an individual present in Canada at the time of supply. Study-abroad placement and immigration/visa consultation services supplied remotely to non-resident clients outside Canada generally qualify for zero-rating provided the supplier retains evidence of non-residency such as passport copy, foreign address, offshore retainer, and foreign payment records. Services physically rendered to an individual in Canada or services tied to Canadian property remain standard-rated at 13% HST.

  • Customs Export Declaration (B13A via CBSA) for any physical export ancillary to study-abroad programmes
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or courier manifest evidencing export movement of goods or application packages
  • Inward remittance certificate or wire-transfer confirmation from a Canadian or foreign bank evidencing receipt of foreign consideration for the zero-rated supply

Canada does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping of invoices. CRA requires sufficient electronic or paper invoice records and accepts GST/HST filing through certified software, GST Access, or NETFILE. There is no API-based pre-clearance mandate for invoice approval; escrow, wire-transfer, and platform transaction records must be retained to evidence taxable consideration.

CRA may deny zero-rating and reassess within the normal four-year period; where false statements or gross negligence are established, the reassessment window is unlimited. Penalties include a gross-negligence penalty of 50% of understated net tax, interest, disallowance of related input tax credits, administrative penalties for false zero-rating documentation, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion. A retrospective clawback restores the 13% HST liability on the invoiced amount, including the 8% Ontario provincial component, when export substantiation is deficient.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains a freely floating exchange rate with full current-account and capital-account convertibility. No exchange control approval is required for inbound equity injection, shareholder loans, service payments, dividend distribution, or repatriation of profits to non-residents. Restrictions are limited to FINTRAC reporting thresholds, cross-border currency declaration obligations, and sanctions. Profit remittances are subject to Canadian withholding tax under the Income Tax Act (Canada) but are not blocked or subject to quota.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canada does not impose statutory surrender requirements or mandatory conversion ratios for export proceeds. A Study Abroad and Immigration Visa consultancy may retain all foreign currency receipts in CAD or foreign currency accounts and convert them at commercial rates without regulatory timeline. Obligations are limited to PCMLTFA reporting for cross-border movements of currency or monetary instruments exceeding CAD 10,000 and FINTRAC large-transaction reporting where applicable.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada); prudential supervision coordinated with the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI); anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing oversight by the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC); economic sanctions administered by Global Affairs Canada.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs and payment fintechs such as Wise, Airwallex, and Payoneer are feasible as supplementary collection and disbursement channels, not as primary liquidity or escrow rails. These entities are not Canadian chartered banks; they operate as Money Services Businesses (MSBs) registered with FINTRAC and may require provincial MSB licensing. They cannot accept trust or escrow deposits under Bank Act powers, do not provide CDIC deposit insurance, and remain dependent on partner-bank settlement capacity. Feasibility rating: Medium for operational receivables/payables; Low as a sole or escrowed-funds solution due to legal and risk constraints.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections should be executed via SWIFT MT103 wires with explicit narrative fields designating 'Equity Injection' or 'Shareholder Loan', supported by an Ontario board resolution, updated shareholder registry, and source-of-funds package. Purpose codes should follow the remitting bank's acceptable trade category (typically investment income or capital transfer) and the receiving bank's product code for inward foreign direct investment. Verification includes SWIFT gpi tracking, beneficiary name and account matching, sanctions screening at both sides, and FINTRAC reporting for transactions exceeding CAD 10,000.
