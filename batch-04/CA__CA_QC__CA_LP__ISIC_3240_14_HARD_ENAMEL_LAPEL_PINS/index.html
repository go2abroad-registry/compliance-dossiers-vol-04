Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Federal: Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Agence du revenu du Canada (ARC) — GST/HST and federal income tax administration. Provincial: Revenu Québec (RQ) — Québec Sales Tax (QST), provincial income tax and payroll accounts. Corporate registry: Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ). Portals: CRA 'My Business Account' and Business Registration Online (BRO); RQ 'Mon dossier d'entreprise' and the joint 'CliquezAffaires' registration service.

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1: Register the Québec société en commandite (CA_LP) with the Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ) via the 'CliquezAffaires' portal; file the initial declaration and the limited partnership registration (name, Québec registered office, identity of all général partners (commandités), a resident Québec attorney for service if the general partner is non-resident) and obtain the Numéro d'entreprise du Québec (NEQ). Step 2: Obtain the federal 9-digit Business Number (BN) through CRA Business Registration Online connected to the NEQ; open the GST/HST program account (RC) and, where applicable, payroll and import/export (RID) program accounts. Step 3: Register with Revenu Québec for the QST account and provincial income/employer accounts using the NEQ; registration is mandatory once taxable supplies in Canada during the preceding four consecutive calendar quarters exceed CAD 30,000 (small-supplier threshold is aligned between GST/HST and QST), or voluntarily to claim input tax credits/refunds (ITCs/ITRs). Step 4: The CA_LP is fiscally transparent; the partnership itself files the federal T5013 and Québec CO-17 information returns and issues Relevé 15 slips to partners. Non-resident limited partners are not registered for a Canadian TIN with respect to the LP, but must provide their foreign TIN on the allocation slips and file Canadian federal (T1, T2 or T1-NR) and Québec income returns for income from the Canadian business; treaty-based relief from Part XIII withholding requires CRA Form NR301/NR302. Step 5: For e-commerce channels (Etsy Direct Pay, Stripe), the LP's BN/QST numbers must appear on all sales invoices issued to Canadian purchasers and on zero-rated export invoices.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Pass_Through_Eligible — the CA_LP is statutorily transparent under s. 96 of the Income Tax Act (Canada) and ss. 598 et seq. of the Québec Taxation Act; income, losses, tax credits and taxable capital gains flow through to partners in each fiscal period. The partnership is not an income taxpayer at the entity level, but is itself a registrant/person for GST/HST and QST purposes on taxable supplies and must collect and remit indirect tax in its own capacity.

        
Available election forms
Canada and Québec do not provide a US-style 'check-the-box' election for domestic partnerships; transparency is mandatory. Pertinent elections include: (1) s. 97(2) ITA / s. 257 QTA rollover election on contribution of appreciated property to the partnership at cost, deferring capital gain; (2) s. 96(3) ITA and corresponding Québec election deeming the partnership not resident in Canada for a fiscal period; (3) s. 249.1 ITA / Québec 'exercice financier' election to adopt an alternate fiscal year-end; (4) a US limited partner may file US Form 8832 to elect the classification of the Canadian LP as a partnership or disregarded entity for US federal tax purposes; (5) hybrid mismatch rules (ss. 18.4(3) and 18.6 ITA; s. 282 QTA) may disallow deductions in respect of hybrid instruments/entities and structured arrangements between the Canadian LP and its US/foreign partners.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
14.975%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
CAD 30,000 — the federal/Québec small-supplier threshold, measured on worldwide taxable supplies made in Canada during the immediately preceding four consecutive calendar quarters, aggregated with associated persons. Registration for GST/HST and QST is due within 29 days after the day the threshold is exceeded. Non-resident vendors selling digital/e-commerce goods into Canadian consumers (including via Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe links) are subject to the 2021 digital-economy deemed-registration rules once the same CAD 30,000 threshold is exceeded.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Zero-rating applies by statute to exports of tangible personal property (hard enamel lapel pins) and to certain offshore services. GST/HST: ETA Schedule VI, Part V (goods exported by supplier or by non-resident recipient; property placed with a carrier for dispatch outside Canada; exported in bond; or destroyed in transit) and s. 7 of Part V for services supplied to a non-resident not performed in Canada in connection with Canadian real estate or tangible property. QST: QST Act Schedule VI, Part V mirrors the GST zero-rating conditions. The CA_LP must demonstrate, contemporaneously, physical export from Canada with reasonable dispatch, a non-resident buyer disposition, and full documentation of the export chain; when so evidenced, the sale is invoiced at 0% GST / 0% QST with full recovery (ITC/ITR) of input tax on materials and services used in producing the exported pins.

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory API stamping, or continuous transaction controls exist in Canada; GST/HST and QST operate on self-assessment with electronic filing through CRA GST/HST NETFILE and Revenu Québec's 'Transmission électronique'. Prescribed invoice content is mandatory (ETA ss. 221(1)-(2) and SOR/91-45 registrations; QST Act ss. 219-232): seller legal name and address, BN/GST registration number and NEQ/QST number, date, description, quantity, unit price, tax amounts and the stated GST/QST rates. The Government of Canada has phased in mandatory Pan-Canadian Peppol (PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0) e-invoicing for suppliers to federal departments (full mandate by 2026), while Revenu Québec offers voluntary EDI/API integration (e.g., 'Relais électronique') but does not require government clearance.

      
Export zero-rating claims are subject to dedicated CRA Export Verification audits and Revenu Québec business-verification reviews. If the CA_LP cannot substantiate physical export with a completed B13A/ACI report, B/L or AWB, and remittance/evidence matching, CRA/Revenu Québec will reassess the sales at 14.975% (GST+QST), disallow the related ITCs/ITRs, and assess: (i) compound daily interest on arrears (ETA s. 280; QSTA s. 1163); (ii) a 25% penalty for false statements or omissions (ETA s. 285(1); QSTA s. 1139.2); (iii) a 50% gross-negligence penalty on tax understated (ETA s. 285.1; QSTA s. 1139.3); (iv) a failure-to-file penalty up to 8% of unpaid tax plus 2% per complete month (ETA s. 280(2)); and (v) criminal prosecution for tax evasion under ETA s. 327 / QSTA s. 1144, carrying summary-conviction fines up to CAD 25,000 and imprisonment to 12 months, or indictment fines from 50% to 200% of the evaded tax and imprisonment to five years. The ordinary four-year reassessment window (ETA s. 298(4); QSTA s. 1147) is unlimited where misrepresentation is attributable to neglect, carelessness or wilful default; retrospective clawback also arises if exported pins return to Canada or are diverted into the Canadian market without full GST/QST accounting at import.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Canada maintains a fully convertible currency with no general exchange control legislation; both current account and capital account transactions are liberalized. Profit remittance is unrestricted at the exchange-control level, but outbound payments to non-residents must comply with the Canadian Income Tax Act, including potential Part XIII withholding tax on deemed dividends, interest, royalties, and certain fees. For a Quebec Limited Partnership, careful characterization of distributions to foreign partners is required to avoid treaty-eligible withholding leakage; no special permit or Bank of Canada approval is required for dividend or capital repatriation.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No mandatory surrender rule applies to export proceeds in Canada. Exporters may hold foreign currency in Canadian or foreign bank accounts, sell it to any authorized dealer at market rates, and use it freely for offshore payments; no statutory timeline for conversion or forced sale is imposed on hard enamel lapel pin exporters.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada) with the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) as the primary prudential regulator and Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC) for anti-money laundering supervision

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Compliant inbound capital injections must be cleared through the Bank of Canada Large Value Transfer System (LVTS) or, for correspondent rails, via SWIFT MT103 from the foreign remitting bank. The remitter should use SWIFT transaction type 'CA' and remittance information coded on the MT103 as 'TRF / CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / LP INTEREST PAYMENT' with clear beneficiary naming of the Quebec Limited Partnership and its operating account. The credit to the LP ledger must attach the executed partnership subscription agreement, source-of-wealth evidence from the remitter, and if applicable a Canadian cross-border method declaration through the Bank of Canada's CF1 form or equivalent bank questionnaire.
