Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Canada does not provide a check-the-box election. A limited partnership is statutorily fiscally transparent under the Income Tax Act and Quebec Taxation Act; no election is available to treat the LP as an association or entity subject to income tax. For hybrid mismatch and foreign classification issues, Canadian domestic law applies OECD BEPS Action 2-based rules; no elective reclassification is permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14.975%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 in worldwide taxable supplies (excluding zero-rated exports) in any calendar quarter or across four consecutive calendar quarters for both GST/HST and QST; digital-service suppliers with Canadian recipients must register if the threshold is met

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: exported goods are zero-rated if they are removed from Canada by the supplier, the purchaser, or a common carrier acting on their behalf, and export documentation establishes shipment abroad. Services and intangible personal property, such as cloud portfolio-tracking software: a supply to a non-resident recipient may be zero-rated if the recipient is not a GST/HST registrant, the supply is not consumed or used in Canada, is not in relation to Canadian real property or tangible personal property located in Canada, and is not supplied to an individual physically present in Canada. However, supplies that constitute 'financial services' are GST/HST/QST-exempt rather than zero-rated; if the app is characterized as providing financial or investment advice or trading execution, its export may not qualify for zero-rating and input-tax credits may be restricted.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CBSA B13A) for goods exported from Canada
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued to a foreign consignee or destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, bank SWIFT/payment receipt, or foreign currency credit statement evidencing the export price
  • Service contract, IP license, user-location evidence and electronic audit trail for digital or offshore services

No mandatory electronic invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance/API stamping regime exists at the federal level or in Quebec. Electronic documents may be used if they maintain integrity, but CRA and Revenu Québec do not require pre-clearance or digital tax stamps. Platform operators and payment service providers retain statutory record-keeping and reporting obligations for taxable activity.

CRA and Revenu Québec may disallow zero-rating and reassess on a nexus or documentary basis; the normal reassessment window is four years, extended in cases of misrepresentation or neglect. Penalties include interest, false-statement penalties up to 50% of the tax at issue, 100% penalties for false refund or rebate claims in egregious cases, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent export claims or refund fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Canadian dollar is fully convertible on both current and capital account. There is no statutory foreign exchange control regime, no exchange rate approval requirement, and no limitation on foreign ownership of a Quebec limited partnership. Profit remittances to non-resident partners are generally unrestricted; however, the Income Tax Act (Canada) and the Taxation Act (Quebec) may impose Part XIII or equivalent withholding on certain payments such as interest, royalties, management fees, and deemed dividends, subject to applicable tax treaty relief. For a flow-through limited partnership, each partner is taxed on its share of partnership income, and partnership distributions of previously taxed income are not subject to further Canadian withholding.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canada does not impose export proceeds surrender requirements. There is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Exporters and entities receiving foreign currency may retain all receipts in freely convertible foreign currency bank accounts in Canada, use them for global disbursements, or convert them at any licensed financial institution.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada); foreign exchange policy is administered by the Department of Finance Canada under the Currency Act and the Public Currency Exchange Regulations. Operational oversight of money laundering and cross-border transaction reporting is exercised by the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC) under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium. Offshore digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, and Payoneer can support multi-currency operational accounts and cost-effective FX for pass-through payments and low-value disbursements. Legal constraints: EMIs are not Canadian chartered banks and cannot hold regulatory capital or provide credit/escrow services; if an EMI solicits or maintains business in Canada, it must register as a Money Services Business (MSB) with FINTRAC, comply with Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, and may not use the term 'bank'. For the capital escrow function, an EMI is not viable; all upstream equity injections and partner distributions should settle through a tier-1 chartered bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for foreign direct investment into the Quebec LP is via a direct SWIFT MT103 wire transfer to the LP's Canadian operational or capital escrow account held at a tier-1 bank. The remittance information field (Field 70) must contain a structured narration: 'PARTNERSHIP CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / CA-LP [entity identification number] / PARTNER REF [unique investor code]'. Where the receiving bank requires a statistical purpose code, use the Canadian Balance of Payments category for 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' (FDI-CAP) or 'Other Capital Transfer – Partnership' as applicable. The bank will perform name, source-of-funds, and sanctions screening on every contributing limited partner. For capital injections exceeding CA$10,000, FINTRAC large transaction record-keeping obligations apply. Execution protocol: (1) execute capital call notice; (2) collect KYC/KYB documents from each partner; (3) obtain legal opinion on partner capacity; (4) instruct payment via bank's global wire portal; (5) confirm receipt and issue a partnership capital receipt letter.Trade settlements for the SaaS financial-tracking platform are predominantly electronic and service-based rather than merchandise-based. Use SWIFT MT103 for international vendor disbursements (e.g., cloud infrastructure providers, overseas contractors); use domestic EFT (CAN-AE, the Canadian automated clearing settlement system) for payroll and Canadian suppliers; use USD EFT via the U.S. correspondent network for dollar-denominated disbursements such as Apple IAP settlement feeds or Stripe payout clearing. A Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) is not appropriate for intangible software services and should only be considered if the partnership procures physical hardware or licenses requiring documentary collections. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Medium. Offshore digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, and Payoneer can support multi-currency operational accounts and cost-effective FX for pass-through payments and low-value disbursements. Legal constraints: EMIs are not Canadian chartered banks and cannot hold regulatory capital or provide credit/escrow services; if an EMI solicits or maintains business in Canada, it must register as a Money Services Business (MSB) with FINTRAC, comply with Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, and may not use the term 'bank'. For the capital escrow function, an EMI is not viable; all upstream equity injections and partner distributions should settle through a tier-1 chartered bank.
