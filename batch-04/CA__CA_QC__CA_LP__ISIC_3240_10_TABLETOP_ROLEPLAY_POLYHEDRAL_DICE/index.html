Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Quebec limited partnership; the partnership agreement must determine each partner's contributions (art. 2238 CCQ). Contributions of limited partners must be capital (cash or property) and fully documented to preserve the liability shield; no escrow account is statutorily mandated, but the general partner must open a dedicated bank account and satisfy PCMLTFA/KYC client identification rules before accepting contributions.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance from the municipal fire department under the Fire Safety Act and the National Fire Code, limiting Class IB flammable liquids (acetone) to approved safety cabinets with spark-proof electrical equipment; structural load inspection for casting and grinding work benches; CNESST workplace registration with a health and safety prevention program and SDS training before operations; finished sharp-edge polyhedral dice must be packaged with adult-use safety labelling and must not be marketed as children's toys under the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) – GST/HST registration, federal Business Number (BN) and federal tax administration; Revenu Québec – Quebec Sales Tax (QST) and provincial tax filings; Registraire des entreprises du Québec – prior mandatory provincial enterprise registration (NEQ), with the CRA Business Registration Online and Revenu Québec business portals used for account setup.

E-government filing portal

1) Register the Limited Partnership with Registraire des entreprises du Québec to obtain the Quebec Enterprise Number (NEQ). 2) Register through CRA Business Registration Online to obtain the federal 9-digit Business Number and open a GST/HST account program (RC1). 3) Apply separately to Revenu Québec for a QST account unless an eligible partner is elected as the partnership registrant for both taxes. 4) Non-resident partners carrying on business through a Quebec establishment must appoint a valid representative for service of process; tax accounts must be active before any taxable supply is made.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — 1) Register the Limited Partnership with Registraire des entreprises du Québec to obtain the Quebec Enterprise Number (NEQ). 2) Register through CRA Business Registration Online to obtain the federal 9-digit Business Number and open a GST/HST account program (RC1). 3) Apply separately to Revenu Québec for a QST account unless an eligible partner is elected as the partnership registrant for both taxes. 4) Non-resident partners carrying on business through a Quebec establishment must appoint a valid representative for service of process; tax accounts must be active before any taxable supply is made.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists for Canadian partnerships; income, gains, losses and QST input tax refunds flow through to partners for federal and Quebec income tax purposes. Relevant elections include: (i) partnership GST/HST registrant election under subsection 273(1) of the Excise Tax Act and the parallel Quebec QST partnership election; (ii) partnership non-calendar fiscal period election under subsection 249.1(4) of the Income Tax Act; and (iii) limited transitional elections on the formation or acquisition of a partnership. Canada applies OECD hybrid mismatch rules under sections 18.4 and 18.5 of the Income Tax Act rather than an elective hybrid mismatch regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14.975%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 in worldwide taxable supplies in a single calendar quarter or across the preceding four consecutive calendar quarters (small supplier threshold); non-resident persons soliciting business in Canada may be required to register regardless of turnover. The QST small supplier threshold is aligned at CAD 30,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A GST/HST and QST registrant may zero-rate handmade epoxy resin RPG dice (tangible personal property) when the goods are physically exported from Canada by the supplier, delivered to a common carrier for export, or sent by mail/courier to a foreign destination, provided the supplier possesses documented proof of export within the required period. Zero-rated exports allow full input tax credits (ITCs) and QST input tax refunds (ITRs) without charging GST/QST. Canada generally does not impose a fixed statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day benchmark is used for export substantiation and financing documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CBSA Form B13A where required)
  • Commercial invoice / Canada Customs Invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Carrier certificate or courier shipment proof
  • Packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank remittance or payment proceeds record

Canada does not impose mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based transaction reporting, or digital stamping. Peppol-based e-invoicing is voluntary. Registered suppliers must issue commercial invoices containing prescribed GST/HST/QST information and retain them for at least six years. Revenu Québec may require electronic transmission of invoice data during an audit.

CRA and Revenu Québec can deny zero-rating, reassess GST/QST, and charge interest plus civil penalties where export documentation is incomplete or inaccurate. Knowing false statements may trigger a 50% civil penalty (e.g., Excise Tax Act subsection 285(1)), criminal fines of CAD 25,000 to CAD 100,000 and imprisonment (Excise Tax Act subsection 289(1)), and parallel QST penalties. The ordinary reassessment window may be extended indefinitely in cases of misrepresentation, and fraudulent export zero-rating claims remain subject to retrospective clawback.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains a fully convertible currency under the Currency Act. No exchange control approval or licensing is required for current or capital account transactions. Partnership capital contributions, profit distributions, royalty payments, and management fees may be remitted cross-border freely, subject only to income tax withholding under Part XIII of the Income Tax Act and any applicable tax treaty (e.g., Canada–US Treaty). Dividend and interest withholding rates are typically reduced for treaty residents; no separate profit-remittance permit exists. Transfer pricing requirements apply to related-party cross-border payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Canada imposes no mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds. Foreign currency receipts may be retained, deposited in foreign currency accounts, or converted at market rates without regulatory approval. No statutory timeline applies to remitting export earnings into the domestic banking system.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (central bank), Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (prudential regulation), Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (AML/FT reporting), and Quebec's Autorité des marchés financiers (securities/registration requirements for underlying investment solicitation, if any).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium. Offshore digital EMI licences (e.g., UK FCA EMI) are not recognised as bank licences in Canada. Such entities cannot accept deposits or provide intra-Canada clearing without registration as a money service business (MSB) under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act and registration with FINTRAC. Wallet-based fintech platforms (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, Stripe Treasury) operate only as payment intermediary systems with fund-safeguarding limitations. They are viable as secondary disbursement and crowdfunding payout rails, but the primary corporate treasury, capital escrow, and FX clearing functions must reside with a Schedule I bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections are executed via high-value SWIFT MT103 messages to the Quebec-domiciled operating account. The remitter must use the following narrative: 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – [LP NAME] – QC REGISTRATION NO. [NEQ]'. For USD-participating LPs, use a US correspondent bank path through the Canadian bank's USD account at a US clearing institution. All inbound wires greater than CAD 10,000 are reportable as electronic fund transfers to FINTRAC. The bank must code the purpose economically as 'Contribution to partnership equity'; no exchange control permit is required. For payments routed through PayPal, remittances are received as a PayPal batch settlement to the Canadian bank account, and the underlying donor name must be shared with the bank if requested for source-of-funds verification.Supplier settlements for raw materials and contract manufacturing are cleared using SWIFT MT103 (telegraphic transfer) for amounts above CAD 25,000, and ACSS (Automated Clearing Settlement System) or Lynx batch EFT for domestic payments.
