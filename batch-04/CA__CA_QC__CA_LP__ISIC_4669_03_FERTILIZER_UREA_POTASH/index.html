Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Revenu Québec (for GST/HST and QST registration in Quebec); CRA portal: My Business Account; Revenu Québec portal: Clic Revenu

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity carrying on business in Canada must register for a CRA Business Number (BN) and, if engaged in taxable activities in Quebec, register with Revenu Québec for a QST number. Application can be made online, by mail, or via authorized representative. The partnership must appoint a representative for service in Canada if it is a non-resident partnership. Registration must occur before filing returns or remitting taxes; GST/HST and QST registrations are effective from the date the entity commences commercial activity, subject to the small supplier threshold.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign entity carrying on business in Canada must register for a CRA Business Number (BN) and, if engaged in taxable activities in Quebec, register with Revenu Québec for a QST number. Application can be made online, by mail, or via authorized representative. The partnership must appoint a representative for service in Canada if it is a non-resident partnership. Registration must occur before filing returns or remitting taxes; GST/HST and QST registrations are effective from the date the entity commences commercial activity, subject to the small supplier threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

A partnership is not a taxpayer for federal income tax purposes; each partner reports share of income. For GST/HST, a partnership is treated as a person and must register if taxable supplies exceed $30,000; a non-resident partnership may elect to register voluntarily. Quebec QST rules mirror GST/HST with certain differences; a partnership may elect to have Revenu Québec qualify its provincial election where permitted. Check-the-box elections are not applicable under Canadian law; however, hybrid mismatch rules under the Income Tax Act may impose entity characterization adjustments, and a Canadian partnership can elect to make certain fiscal period selections (e.g., non-calendar fiscal year) that affect deferral.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14.975%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 of worldwide taxable supplies over four consecutive calendar quarters for GST/HST; the QST threshold corresponds to the same $30,000 threshold applicable to taxable supplies in Quebec, subject to Revenu Québec rules

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated under GST/HST and QST applies to supplies of goods that are exported by the vendor, including sales of fertilizer products sold to non-residents for export provided the vendor retains evidence of export. A supply of goods is zero-rated where the goods are delivered by the vendor to a common carrier for export, or shipped to a destination outside Canada, and the supplier has satisfactory documentary evidence that the goods were exported. Services performed on goods destined for export may also be zero-rated if the service is performed for a non-resident. The export of goods by a licensed customs broker under the sales contract qualifies.

  • Canada Customs Export Declaration (B13A / Canadian Automated Export Declaration - CAED)
  • Bill of Lading / Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of payment from foreign buyer
  • Sales invoice and contract demonstrating the goods were sold for export
  • Customs entry or release documentation in the importing jurisdiction (where available)

Canada does not mandate a real-time e-invoicing clearance system; electronic invoicing is authorized but must contain prescribed information. Quebec mandates the use of the Revenu Québec digital attestation and standardized electronic form for QST registrants. There is no national real-time tax stamping or API clearance requirement similar to certain Latin American jurisdictions; however, businesses must report prescribed information through the CRA's GST/HST NETFILE for electronic filings and via Revenu Québec's electronic filing services.

False or misleading export claims give rise to penalties under the Excise Tax Act, including penalties equal to 5% of the net tax falsely reported plus up to 20% of the amounts claimed in respect of non-compliance. CRA may reassess an input tax credit or zero-rated treatment at any time within the normal reassessment period of four years, with extended periods for misrepresentation. Revenu Québec imposes parallel penalties under the Taxation Act, including 5% plus 20% administrative penalties for false QST claims; criminal prosecution is possible for intentional evasion. The CRA may deny zero-rated treatment retroactively and assess net tax, interest, and penalties if the exporter cannot substantiate the export with required documentation within the stipulated retention period of six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Canadian dollar is fully convertible under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. There are no exchange control restrictions on current account transactions or capital account transactions, including inbound capital injections, trade finance settlements, and profit remittances. A Quebec limited partnership may freely receive foreign currency and convert to CAD at any authorized bank. The principal hurdle on outbound distributions to non-resident partners is Part XIII withholding tax under the Income Tax Act on amounts allocated to non-residents, typically at 25% absent a tax treaty reduction; repatriation of contributed capital is unrestricted. Banks may require completion of a non-resident declaration and tax form to confirm treaty eligibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canada imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio. A Quebec-based LP engaged in wholesale urea and potash trade may retain export receipts in USD or other currencies. Transactions of CAD 10,000 or more are reported to FINTRAC by the financial intermediary as an electronic funds transfer report. Exports are also subject to record-keeping obligations under the Customs Act and Canada Border Services Agency reporting, but no CAD conversion is required.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada); responsibility for monetary policy and the national payment system. Canada does not maintain a distinct Foreign Exchange Administration; exchange controls and cross-border capital regulations are administered under the Bank of Canada Act, the Currency Act, and the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act, with the Department of Finance Canada holding policy authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium feasibility for ancillary treasury flows only. Canadian law does not recognize EU Electronic Money Institution (EMI) passporting. Fintech payment firms such as Wise and Airwallex may operate as Money Services Businesses (MSBs) in Canada and must be registered with FINTRAC. They are viable for low-value payment aggregation, cross-border vendor payments, and FX execution at mid-market rates, but they cannot issue Canadian-dollar deposit accounts, provide regulated commercial credit, or issue documentary letters of credit. The primary clearing rails for fertilizer commodity trade must remain with Tier-1 domestic commercial banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions from non-resident partners must be wired via SWIFT MT103/MT202 to the LP's CAD operating account held at a Canadian Tier-1 bank. CAD-denominated large-value transfers settle in real time on the Payments Canada Lynx system; USD-denominated injections clear through correspondent banking arrangements or via CLS for same-day FX. The wire beneficiary field must include the LP legal name, Quebec enterprise number (NEQ), the phrase 'capital contribution', and must not indicate a taxable purchase. FINTRAC EFT reporting will apply to transactions of CAD 10,000 or more; the bank will verify the source of funds against the LP agreement and beneficial ownership register.For settlement of urea and potash imports, the preferred outbound clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for outright telegraphic transfer. For deferred payment or guarantee of delivery, issue an irrevocable commercial letter of credit via SWIFT MT700 under UCP600, with confirming bank participation as requested. USD-denominated payments may be routed through the bank's USD clearing accounts or CHIPS; CAD payments use the Lynx high-value system. All instructions must include the commercial invoice, weight certificate, port of loading, and bill of lading.
