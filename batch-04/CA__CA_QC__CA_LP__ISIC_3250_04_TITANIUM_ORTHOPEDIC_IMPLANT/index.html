Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital or escrow account requirement under Québec law. Each limited partner must contribute the amount or asset stated in the partnership contract (CCQ art. 2240); a limited partner who withdraws its contribution remains liable to third-party creditors for two years (CCQ art. 2250).

Funding & unlock path: Municipal fire safety permit and inspection covering electron-beam vacuum furnace hazards; structural load clearance signed by a licensed Québec Professional Engineer (OIQ) for 3D printers and vacuum chambers; CNESST workplace compliance clearance (machine guarding, respiratory protection, electrical safety); municipal building and occupancy permits with zoning conformity.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for federal GST/HST and income tax accounts; Revenu Québec for Québec sales tax (QST) and provincial tax filings. Portals: CRA Business Registration Online (BRO) / My Business Account; Revenu Québec Online services (ClicSÉQ).

E-government filing portal

A foreign limited partnership registers through CRA Business Registration Online (BRO) to obtain a 9-digit Business Number (BN) with an RT program account for GST/HST; for QST registration, it files through Revenu Québec's CliCCh? CRA may require the non-resident partnership to designate a representative or agent in Canada. Revenu Québec subsequently issues the Québec enterprise number (NEQ) and QST registration; registration is effective as of the date the partnership first makes a taxable supply in Canada or Québec, whichever applies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

A Canadian limited partnership is fiscally transparent for federal and provincial income tax purposes; it files a T5013 partnership information return and normally does not pay federal or Quebec income tax at entity level. Available elections include: (i) selection of a partnership fiscal period under paragraph 249.1(1)(b) of the Income Tax Act; (ii) GST/HST and QST election to have a one-time fiscal year change under section 241 of the Excise Tax Act with Quebec equivalent; (iii) elections under the anti-hybrid rules in section 20.2 of the Income Tax Act and the related amendments to hybrid mismatch arrangements; (iv) entity classification elections applicable only to certain trusts or partnerships for U.S. check-the-box purposes, not domestic Canadian elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14.975%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

$30,000 CAD (annual worldwide taxable supplies threshold for small supplier status under the Excise Tax Act and Quebec Sales Tax Act; once exceeded, the partnership must register for GST/HST and QST)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

GST/HST and QST zero-rating applies to the supply of titanium orthopedic implants exported by the supplier or by the non-resident purchaser, provided the goods are shipped out of Canada, supported by adequate proof of export, and the supplier retains records showing the export occurred. The zero-rating eligiblity includes deliveries to a common carrier or courier for export, delivery to a freight forwarder for export, or direct export by the purchaser under a written contract stipulating export within 60 days. In the case of offshore services to non-residents (e.g., warranties, repair services) zero-rating may apply only where the service result is performed outside Canada or in furtherance of an export; all supporting documents must be maintained.

  • Canadian Export Declaration (B13) filed under the Canada Customs Export Reporting System (CAED)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical shipment to the non-resident
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/heel? bank statement evidencing remittance of export proceeds

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping in Canada; electronic invoicing is voluntary, with open PEPPOL adoption. Revenu Québec may require XML invoices for certain registered suppliers, but for titanium orthopedic implant manufacturing there is no fiscal clearance API requirement. Businesses must retain electronic records and provide access to the CRA and Revenu Québec on demand.

The CRA and Revenu Québec may deny zero-rating and claw back input tax credits if insufficient proof of export is maintained; penalties include a $250 administrative penalty or 10% of the rebate claimed under section 285.1 of the Excise Tax Act for false statements, plus interest and late-payment penalties. Wilful attempts to evade tax or knowingly provide false information constitute criminal offences with fines of not less than 50% and up to 200% of the tax sought to be evaded and imprisonment up to five years. Revenu Québec imposes parallel penalties under the Tax Administration Act (Chapter 1, s. 31.5), including civil penalties for omission, negligent misstatements, or false declarations; assessments may be reopened within the normal four-year period, extended to seven years for misrepresentation and unlimited for fraud.
