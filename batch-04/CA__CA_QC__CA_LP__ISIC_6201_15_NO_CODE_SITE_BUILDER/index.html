Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible for REQ registration purposes only if a physical street address in Quebec is designated as the LP's establishment (post-office-box-only addresses are inadmissible); a commercial virtual office suite with reception and mail handling is accepted by REQ; however, FINTRAC-regulated payment processors and the general partner's duty to maintain accounting records and the limited partners' register at an accessible Quebec address make a physical or counsel-supervised address advisable; the general partner bears unlimited liability and must maintain substantive records.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required for a Quebec limited partnership; the partnership agreement exclusively determines capital contributions, which may be in cash, in services, or in property. No escrow account or government deposit is required.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable to the digital business itself. If a commercial office or co-working space is occupied, the LP must obtain municipal occupancy and fire safety conformity attestations from the competent fire safety authority, ensure electrical and structural load compliance for any leasehold improvements, and, where employees are recruited, register with the Commission des normes, de l'équité, de la santé et de la sécurité du travail (CNESST) and implement a workplace health and safety prevention program.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required for a Quebec limited partnership; the partnership agreement exclusively determines capital contributions, which may be in cash, in services, or in property. No escrow account or government deposit is required.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) — Business Registration Online (BRO) and My Business Account (canada.ca/cra-business-registration); Revenu Québec — Entreprise Revenu Québec portal for Quebec Sales Tax (QST), source deductions, and provincial registrations (revenuquebec.ca). The Quebec enterprise number is issued by the Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ).

E-government filing portal

For a Quebec Limited Partnership (société en commandite), no separate income-tax entity number is issued because income tax is assessed on partners. The statutory tax identification consists of the CRA Business Number (BN) and the Revenu Québec file/QST number. Procedure: (1) register the extra-provincial limited partnership or the non-resident general partner with the Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ) to obtain the Quebec establishment number; (2) register the partnership for a CRA Business Number through Business Registration Online, selecting the GST/HST account and, if applicable, payroll account; (3) within the same CRA registration flow, elect to register with Revenu Québec for QST and provincial source-deduction accounts; (4) appoint a representative with a Canadian nexus for service of notices and returns; (5) retain the CRA BN and RQ file number as the VAT/TIN identifiers for GST/HST and QST purposes respectively. Non-resident partners may also need to provide Form RC519 or equivalent information to document their Canadian contact; Stripe and Paddle onboarding will require the BN and QST numbers for Quebec-based sales.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Canada does not operate a check-the-box entity-classification regime. A limited partnership is statutorily transparent for income tax; each partner includes its share of income/loss. No entity-level election can make the partnership a corporation. Available elections are limited to ancillary timing/allocation matters, such as partnership fiscal-period elections under Income Tax Act subsection 96(3), and the partner-level choice to treat a foreign partnership as transparent or opaque under the foreign-entity classification rules when cross-border hybrid elements are present. Hybrid mismatch consequences are addressed through the OECD MLI and the Canadian hybrid mismatch rules (Part VI.1 and related provisions); there is no elective regime to alter the default pass-through classification for Canadian income tax purposes. For GST/HST and QST, the partnership itself is a registerable person and may not elect to be disregarded.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: No automatic statutory audit for a Quebec limited partnership carrying on a low-risk software business. An audit is compulsory if: (a) the LP or its general partner is a reporting issuer under Quebec securities law or NI 51-102; (b) the LP is an investment plan within ITA s. 149.1(1)(d.1) or s. 197; (c) the partnership agreement or a lender covenant requires an audit (typical where limited partners exceed 100 or non-resident capital is material); or (d) the general partner itself is subject to a statutory audit. Absent these triggers, a review engagement (CPA Canada Handbook Section 8200) or notice-to-reader compilation is sufficient.
