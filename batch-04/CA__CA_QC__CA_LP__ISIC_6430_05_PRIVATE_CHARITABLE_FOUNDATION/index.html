Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) — Business Registration Online and GST/HST NETFILE; Revenu Québec — online services and CléNEQ; Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ) — Registre des entreprises online registry.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned limited partnership carrying on activities in Canada and Québec: (i) register the limited partnership with the Registraire des entreprises du Québec to obtain a Quebec establishment number; (ii) obtain a CRA Business Number (BN) with GST/HST, payroll, and import/export program accounts through Business Registration Online or Form RC1; (iii) register for QST with Revenu Québec once taxable supplies are made in Québec and the small-supplier threshold is exceeded, unless only exempt supplies are made; (iv) because the partnership is fiscally transparent, the partnership files an information return (T5013) under its BN but does not pay federal income tax on partnership income; each partner reports its allocated share; (v) a non-resident partner carrying on business in Canada may require a Canadian non-resident tax account and should file the appropriate Part I or Part XIII election/return; (vi) if the private charitable foundation is itself a corporation or trust, CRA charitable registration must be obtained separately, and the GST/QST registration of that charity may be administered under its own BN.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — For a foreign-owned limited partnership carrying on activities in Canada and Québec: (i) register the limited partnership with the Registraire des entreprises du Québec to obtain a Quebec establishment number; (ii) obtain a CRA Business Number (BN) with GST/HST, payroll, and import/export program accounts through Business Registration Online or Form RC1; (iii) register for QST with Revenu Québec once taxable supplies are made in Québec and the small-supplier threshold is exceeded, unless only exempt supplies are made; (iv) because the partnership is fiscally transparent, the partnership files an information return (T5013) under its BN but does not pay federal income tax on partnership income; each partner reports its allocated share; (v) a non-resident partner carrying on business in Canada may require a Canadian non-resident tax account and should file the appropriate Part I or Part XIII election/return; (vi) if the private charitable foundation is itself a corporation or trust, CRA charitable registration must be obtained separately, and the GST/QST registration of that charity may be administered under its own BN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Canada has no US-style check-the-box election; a limited partnership cannot elect to be taxed as a corporation. Available mechanisms include: (i) allocation of income or loss among partners in accordance with the limited partnership agreement, subject to fiscal reasonableness; (ii) voluntary GST/HST registration by a small supplier and voluntary QST registration to claim input tax credits or rebates where available; (iii) an election under the Income Tax Act for a non-resident partner to file a Canadian income tax return on its allocable Canadian-source income; and (iv) application of Canadian hybrid mismatch rules only to deny a deduction or include an amount where a foreign jurisdiction treats the arrangement inconsistently, with no elective override.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14.975%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 small-supplier threshold for both GST/HST and QST; registration is mandatory once taxable supplies exceed CAD 30,000, unless the supplies are exempt such as most financial services. A non-resident carrying on business in Canada may be required to register even below the threshold if the CRA or Revenu Québec determines it is carrying on a taxable commercial activity in Canada; voluntary registration is available to recover input tax credits or public service body rebates.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated at 0% GST/QST when exported from Canada and documentary evidence proves delivery outside Canada. Services may be zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident and not consumed in Canada, or when performed in respect of exported goods. Financial services relating to charitable endowment management are generally exempt rather than zero-rated, unless the supply is an exported supply of a financial service made to a non-resident for use wholly outside Canada; in all cases the supplier must retain contemporaneous contracts, invoices, and payment records establishing the foreign destination.

  • CERS electronic export declaration (Canadian Export Reporting System) or CBSA B13A export declaration where applicable.
  • Commercial invoice and endowment management or grant agreement identifying the non-resident counterparty and foreign destination.
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Canada.
  • Foreign exchange inward remittance certificate, SWIFT MT103, or bank account credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency.

Canada and Québec do not impose a mandatory real-time electronic-invoice clearance, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping regime. Suppliers must issue prescribed GST/HST/QST invoices containing supplier name, GST/QST registration number, invoice date, description of supply, and tax amounts. GST/HST and QST returns are filed electronically where required; CRA and Revenu Québec provide optional APIs and authenticated portals, but no per-invoice fiscal clearance or API stamping is mandated.

CRA and Revenu Québec may deny zero-rating, reassess net tax, and impose prescribed interest compounded daily. Gross negligence penalties of 50% of the understated net tax or overclaimed refund can apply. Knowingly filing a false or fraudulent export zero-rating claim is an offence punishable by fines up to CAD 25,000 on summary conviction, and tax evasion penalties may reach 200% of the amount evaded with potential imprisonment. The normal four-year reassessment window is extended where the taxpayer has made a misrepresentation or filed a knowingly false return.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility of CAD on both current and capital accounts; no exchange control, no authorization, and no repatriation approval is required for capital injections, endowment contributions, donations, or outward grant/disbursement flows under the Bank of Canada Act, Currency Act, or any foreign exchange decree. Practical hurdles for this Quebec limited partnership endowment vehicle are: (i) the underlying charitable foundation must hold CRA/Revenu Québec registration and meet the annual disbursement quota (3.5% of non-qualified investment assets); (ii) Income Tax Act s.149.1(6) requires that at least 50% of the charity's directors be Canadian residents; (iii) the LP's flow-through status exposes non-resident partners to Part XIV Canadian tax and treaty-based withholding on taxable Canadian-source income; and (iv) Investment Canada Act thresholds do not affect passive donations but may screen any non-resident acquisition of a controlling interest in the managing general partner. Profit remittance and foreign donor distributions are otherwise unrestricted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil. Canada imposes no export proceeds surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory holding period for export receipts, foreign donations, or investment proceeds. Foreign currency may be retained indefinitely in USD/EUR/GBP accounts without BOC or AMF approval; conversion is a privately negotiated wholesale or retail FX transaction.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (BOC) as issuer of the Canadian dollar and holder of official international reserves, with OSFI as prudential supervisor for all Schedule I/II banks; the FX control function is largely residual since Canada operates a liberalized IMF Article VIII regime. The Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF) licenses and supervises Quebec-chartered banks and credit unions, Global Affairs Canada administers sanctions (Special Economic Measures Act, UN Act-created measures), and FINTRAC enforces the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA) across all reporting entities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW as the primary banking rail; MEDIUM as an ancillary FX/payment engine. Canada does not recognize the EU/UK statutory 'EMI' license category. Offshore digital e-money institutions cannot hold charity endowment corpus, issue CAD cheques, accept PCMLTFA-prescribed deposit functions, or open transit/institution-numbered accounts without either a Bank Act (Canada) banking license or FINTRAC money services business (MSB) registration. A Quebec LP with charitable endowment status may use a FINTRAC-registered digital EMI (e.g., Wise Business or Payoneer via a Canadian agent) only for low-value pre-funded operational transfers such as seeding small scientific grants or subscription top-ups, never for the statutory capital escrow or donor-restricted corpus. Legal constraints require full sanctions screening of the EMI's correspondent chain and explicit board-approved treasury limits.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Standard compliant protocol: international wires routed via SWIFT MT103 (with MT202 COV where a cover payment is required) using the entity's own transit/institution numbers; domestic CAD wires settled through Payments Canada Lynx (RTGS same-day finality). No invoice is required for donations; credits above CAD 10,000 trigger FINTRAC EFT reporting. All foreign-currency wires are converted by the bank's dealing room at agreed-upon rates, with the daily trade-ticket stored in the treasury file.
