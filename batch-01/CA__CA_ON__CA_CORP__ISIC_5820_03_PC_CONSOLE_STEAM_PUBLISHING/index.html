Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Canadian dollar is fully and freely convertible on both current-account and capital-account transactions. The Exchange Control Act was repealed in 1951, and no licence, prior approval, repatriation quota, or conversion mandate applies to profit remittance, capital injection, or trade settlements. The only profit-repatriation hurdle is tax-based: dividends paid by CA_CORP to non-resident shareholders attract Part XIII withholding tax (statutory 25%, treaty-reduced, typically to 5–15% under Canada's income tax conventions), but the underlying foreign-currency wire is unrestricted. USD revenue received from Valve (Steam) and Sony (PlayStation Store) may be retained, converted, or remitted freely without central-bank clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Canada imposes no statutory surrender, conversion, or repatriation obligation on export proceeds; the historical Exchange Control Act surrender regime was abolished in 1951. CA_CORP may lawfully retain Steam and PlayStation Store USD receipts in a Canadian USD-denominated account or in any offshore account of its Treasury. The sole compliance overlay is FINTRAC's large-EFT reporting threshold of CAD $10,000 or more per cross-border transfer, which the reporting bank discharges at the point of receipt or dispatch; OSFI net-open-currency-position rules apply to the banking counterparty, not to the non-financial corporate exporter.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada) is the central monetary authority responsible for the Canadian dollar and exchange-rate policy. Canada maintains no statutory foreign-exchange control regime; the administrative perimeter is instead covered by the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) for prudential bank supervision, the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC) for anti-money-laundering/anti-terrorist-financing surveillance of cross-border electronic funds transfers, and Payments Canada for the operation of the large-value clearing system (Lynx).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium. A purely offshore electronic money institution (EMI) without Canadian registration cannot clear CAD, participate in Lynx settlement, or offer CDIC-insured deposits, and therefore is not a substitute for a tier-1 operating account. However, FINTRAC-registered Money Services Businesses with Canadian presence (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, OFX) are viably used as overlay rails for collecting USD storefront payouts from Valve and Sony and for executing low-value international disbursements to co-publishers. A Schedule III branch of a foreign bank under the Bank Act is legally permissible but commercially impracticable and capital-inefficient for a mid-market software publisher.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Beneficial ownership opacity: FINTRAC and the Canada Business Corporations Act require identification and verification of every individual with significant control (shareholder or other person who exercises control, typically 25% or more of voting rights or fair-market value), and Ontario's corporate registry rules mandate corresponding reporting.

Root cause: Beneficial ownership opacity: FINTRAC and the Canada Business Corporations Act require identification and verification of every individual with significant control (shareholder or other person who exercises control, typically 25% or more of voting rights or fair-market value), and Ontario's corporate registry rules mandate corresponding reporting.

Defense evidence: Signed Significant Control Persons Register maintained under s. 2.1 CBCA or OBCA; Certificate of Incorporation (Federal or Ontario); Ontario Business Information Report; notarized organizational chart; individual UBO declarations with full name, date of birth, residential address, and shareholding percentage; government-issued photo ID (passport or Ontario driver's licence) for every significant-control individual.
Source-of-wealth and source-of-funds on the initial inbound shareholder capital subscription or shareholder loan.

Root cause: Source-of-wealth and source-of-funds on the initial inbound shareholder capital subscription or shareholder loan.

Defense evidence: Six months of bank statements for the originating shareholder/entity; executed equity subscription agreement or shareholder loan agreement; proof of business legitimacy (business plan, revenue forecast, published storefront pipeline); sworn source-of-funds declaration; and, where the funding party is a non-resident corporation, a tax-residency certificate and a Part XIII withholding-law analysis confirming that injected share capital is not taxable Canadian income.
Recurring large USD credits from foreign digital-platform payers (Valve Corporation, Sony Interactive Entertainment) may be flagged as unverified third-party funding.

Root cause: Recurring large USD credits from foreign digital-platform payers (Valve Corporation, Sony Interactive Entertainment) may be flagged as unverified third-party funding.

Defense evidence: Signed Steamworks Distribution Agreement with Valve; PlayStation Partner digital publishing agreement with Sony; storefront revenue dashboards for the trailing three months; W-8BEN-E (Canada-US tax form) evidencing beneficial-owner status in the US; payout advice statements from each platform; and a corporate treasury resolution identifying the approved payout counterparties.
Cross-border transfer frequency and jurisdiction screening: Steam/PlayStation remittances originate from US, EU, and JP legal entities and may produce false-positive sanctions hits.

Root cause: Cross-border transfer frequency and jurisdiction screening: Steam/PlayStation remittances originate from US, EU, and JP legal entities and may produce false-positive sanctions hits.

Defense evidence: Completed screening of all counterparties against the Consolidated Canadian Autonomous Sanctions List, OFAC SDN List, and EU Consolidated List at account opening and quarterly thereafter; commercial contract extracts proving end-customer and counterparty identity; annual certification by the compliance officer that no sanctioned person owns, controls, or brokers the platform payouts.
Registered-office mismatch, incomplete director mandates, or insufficient signing-authority governance for treasury operations.

Root cause: Registered-office mismatch, incomplete director mandates, or insufficient signing-authority governance for treasury operations.

Defense evidence: Current Ontario Business Information Report reflecting the registered office; board resolution appointing two signing officers with dual-signature authority for all wires above CAD $25,000; specimen signature cards certified by the corporate secretary; and the company's banking resolution form executed in accordance with the CBCA/OBCA by-law.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital is delivered by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank to the CA_CORP tier-1 operational CAD account; the payment narrative must state the company legal name, corporate registration number, and an IMF BPM6-aligned purpose descriptor such as 'equity injection — direct investment' or 'shareholder loan' to avoid mischaracterization as Canadian-source revenue. CAD settlement occurs in real time over the Payments Canada Lynx RTGS system; USD-denominated injections are cleared through the Canadian bank's US correspondent over Fedwire/CHIPS and then spot-converted into CAD. The receiving bank files a FINTRAC Large EFT report for any transaction of CAD $10,000 or more. The treasury must reconcile each credit advice against the subscribed capital ledger and maintain the supporting board resolution to document that share issuance proceeds are credited to contributed surplus rather than taxable income.Inbound digital-store receivables are collected as SWIFT MT103 USD wires from Valve (Steam monthly publisher settlement) and Sony (PlayStation Store partner settlement) into the CA_CORP USD operating account; the treasury then executes a spot USD/CAD FX conversion on the bank platform and funds CAD payroll, tax, and operational obligations via Lynx or internal electronic funds transfer. Outbound trade settlements to co-publishers, middleware licensors, royalty agents, and escrow depositaries are executed by SWIFT MT103 wires with the invoice/reference number quoted in the narrative; for high-value development-advance contracts, a Commercial Letter of Credit (import/export UC) may be opened or confirmed through the bank's trade-finance desk, with compliance with the UCP 600 rules.
